For each bounded step, typed hashes compare the original cursor indices, row dictionaries, input/decoder/target tensors, loss and microbatch sizes, updated model tensors, full AdamW state, full cursor state, CPU/CUDA RNG, and post-update teacher-forced logits. Tensor hashing includes dtype, shape, and exact bytes, preserving signed zero. No state_dict is saved.
